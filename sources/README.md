# Sources

The PDFs are kept locally and excluded from the repository. They're copyrighted
publications. Obtain them through the DOIs below.

## Primary

**Fujiwara, M., Chow, P. S., Ma, D. L., & Braatz, R. D. (2002).**
Paracetamol crystallization using laser backscattering and ATR-FTIR spectroscopy:
metastability, agglomeration, and control. *Crystal Growth & Design*, 2(5), 363–370.
DOI: [10.1021/cg0200098](https://doi.org/10.1021/cg0200098)
Used for: solubility correlation ln C₀ = 5193.109/T + 27.121124 ln T − 178.28055
(C₀ as mole fraction, T in K, fitted 20–50 °C); ΔH\* and Cp\*; seed loading and fill ratio precedent.

## Validation

**Granberg, R. A., & Rasmuson, Å. C. (1999).**
Solubility of paracetamol in pure solvents. *Journal of Chemical & Engineering Data*, 44(6), 1391–1395.
DOI: [10.1021/je990124v](https://doi.org/10.1021/je990124v)
Used for: independent water solubility data, 0–30 °C; solvent comparison (methanol, ethanol).

## Supporting

**Shakeel, F., Alanazi, F. K., Alsarra, I. A., & Haq, N. (2013).**
Solubility of paracetamol in Transcutol + water mixtures, 298.15–333.15 K.
*Journal of Chemical & Engineering Data*. DOI: [10.1021/je4008525](https://doi.org/10.1021/je4008525)
ThermoML data: `Shakeel_2013_ThermoML_je4008525.json` (NIST TRC).
Used for: supporting pure-water series above 30 °C. Not a primary source (cosolvent study).

**Xu, F., Sun, L. X., Tan, Z. C., Liang, J. G., & Zhang, T. (2006).**
Adiabatic calorimetry and thermal analysis on acetaminophen. *J. Therm. Anal. Calorim.*, 83(1), 187–191.
DOI: [10.1007/s10973-005-6969-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10973-005-6969-0)
Used for: Form I heat capacity, Cp,m = 17.645 + 0.5226T (150–330 K), energy balance.

**NIST Chemistry WebBook, SRD 69** (Lemmon, McLinden & Friend), isobaric properties of water at 0.10132 MPa.
Used for: water enthalpy H(T) and Cp, 288.15–323.15 K, energy balance.

**Atlas Steels (2011).** *Grade 316/316L Datasheet*, rev. Jan 2011.
Used for: density (8,000 kg/m³) and specific heat (500 J/kg·K, 0–100 °C) of the vessel shell.

## Background

**Leitner, J., & Sedmidubský, D. (2016).** Enhanced solubility of nanostructured paracetamol.
*Biomed. Phys. Eng. Express*, 2, 055007. DOI: [10.1088/2057-1976/2/5/055007](https://doi.org/10.1088/2057-1976/2/5/055007)
Used for: pointer to Xu (2006); second solubility correlation over 273–323 K (possible check on the 15 °C extrapolation; not yet used).

**Hendriksen, B. A., Grant, D. J. W., Meenan, P., & Green, D. A. (1998).**
Crystallisation of paracetamol (acetaminophen) in the presence of structurally related substances.
*Journal of Crystal Growth*, 183(4), 629–640.
Used for: why the "crystals are pure" assumption is a simplification.

**ICH Q3C: Impurities, Guideline for Residual Solvents.**
Used for: solvent class comparison (methanol Class 2, ethanol Class 3).
